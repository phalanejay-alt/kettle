### Rule 1: Only Sell Pain Relief, Not Products
- Don't sell "URL checking API"
- Sell "Stop phishing leaks in your customer base"
- Sell "Reduce security tickets by 40%"
- Sell "Compliance requirement solved"

### Rule 2: Find Money Before Building
- Talk to 100 potential customers FIRST
- Only build what they'll pay for
- NEVER build something and then find buyers

### Rule 3: Margins Over Volume
- Better: 1 customer at $1,000/month
- Than: 1,000 customers at $1/month
- Easier to service, more stable, faster to exit

### Rule 4: Recurring Revenue Only
- Don't do one-time deals
- Everything must be repeating monthly/yearly
- This compounds into wealth

### Rule 5: Stack Revenue Streams
- Don't rely on one customer
- 5-10 revenue streams = stability
- If one dies, others carry you

### Rule 6: Zero Acquisition Cost is Priority
- Affiliate (they bring customers)
- Resellers (they find their own clients)
- Viral (bots, word of mouth)
- Never buy expensive ads early

### Rule 7: Own Your Infrastructure
- Use free tier (GitHub, Cloudflare, Base)
- No vendor lock-in
- Can pivot quickly
- Margins stay high
